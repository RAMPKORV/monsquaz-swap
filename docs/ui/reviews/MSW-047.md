# UI review — MSW-045 and MSW-047

Review date: 2026-11-26. Build: worktree at the MSW-047 branch.
Screenshots: `docs/ui/screens/` names listed below.

## Scope

- Mission control: lock-ready, round-ready, reveal-ready, offline, and
  reconnecting states.
- Settings: gateway online, offline, reconnecting, and not-started states.
- Lock and reveal confirmation dialogs.
- 1280×800, 1024×700, light theme, and 200% zoom captures.

## Wave 1 — Visual design

| View | State | Severity | Problem | Resolution |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mission + Settings | Reconnecting | must-fix | Footer said “No bot” while the page said “Reconnecting”. | Added the connecting/reconnecting branches to the footer and sidebar. |
| Mission | Reconnecting | should-fix | The warning banner had no warning style. | Added `.banner--warn`. |
| Mission | Reconnecting | should-fix | Bot health used a muted pill. | Reconnecting and connecting now use the warning tone. |
| Mission | Offline | should-fix | The secondary action used the pause icon. | The secondary icon follows the action. |
| Mission | Reveal ready | should-fix | The timeline marked round 3 current. | The reveal-ready fixture marks every round done and reveal current. |
| Settings | Offline | should-fix | A green “Configured” pill sat above a red gateway row. | The header pill now follows gateway health: Connected, Connecting, Reconnecting, Offline, or Token stored. |
| Mission | 1024 | should-fix | Gateway detail was below the fold. | Added a compact gateway pill to the page header. |
| Mission | Lock ready | should-fix | The banner said “Seeds are in” while a decision said two seeds were missing. | The banner states 10 seeds and 2 missing seeds. |

| View | State | Severity | Problem | Resolution |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| Mission | Any | must-fix | Review decisions and decision actions were not wired. | Review decisions scrolls to the decision list. Schedule decisions open the planner. Other decisions show “Soon”. |

| Mission | Reveal ready | must-fix | Reveal had one irreversible click. | Added a confirmation dialog with anonymity and queue consequences. |

| Mission | Lock ready | should-fix | The production projection could not show lock-ready. | `roster_locked` now means an active roster, not any participant. |
| Mission | Round ready | should-fix | The banner did not state the offer count. | The banner states that opening the round sends 10 offers. |
| Mission | Lock ready | should-fix | The timeline marked seeds current before lock. | Seeds are done; roster lock is current. |
| Mission + Settings | Offline | should-fix | The two views gave different causes. | Both use the token rejection text. |
