## Wave 1 — visual design

Reviewer: independent subagent. Verdict: not a pass at first. Must-fixes found:

1. The close-seed action was above the facts and the late-seed warning.
   **Fixed:** seed card moved above the invite card, the card shows a red
   late-seed notice, and the close action is disabled while a late seed waits.
2. The three seed facts left an empty cell in a two-column grid.
   **Fixed:** a three-column modifier at 900 px and wider.
3. The planner matrix was below the fold at 1024×700.
   **Fixed:** the summary stays four-up at 1180 px and narrower; the first
   matrix row is visible at 1024×700.
4. The seed card was visually weaker than the invite card.
   **Fixed:** seed card first, same surface, 16 px title.
5. The late-seed banner was clipped and its copy was narrow.
   **Fixed:** warning copy takes the row, actions sit at the card edge.
6. The Open Round 1 button was bottom-aligned to helper text.
   **Fixed:** the deadline row aligns the button to the input.
7. The disabled Open button failed label contrast in light mode.
   **Fixed:** scoped disabled style keeps a readable label.
8. The seed deadline showed a US-style input value and no timezone.
   **Fixed:** the field help shows the formatted deadline with the IANA zone.
9. Long seed and join copy ran wider than 72–78 characters.
   **Fixed:** seed card subtitle capped at 72 ch; join copy wraps earlier.

Re-check: pass. Screenshots listed above are the final set.

## Wave 2 — UX and copy

Reviewer: independent subagent. Must-fixes found and resolved:

- Event facts now show **Seed round 7 days (Round 1)** and **Exchange round
  24 hours (final 48 hours)**. The classic claim-window fact is removed because
  the classic round has one deadline.
- Deadline copy names the base time: "7 days after you open Round 1".
- Late seeds are visible and gated: the host cannot close Round 1 while a late
  seed waits.
- Closing the seed round now has a confirmation dialog with the seed count and
  the consequences.
- Naming is consistent: **Round 1** in prose, **R2** to **RN** in matrix
  headers, **file v2** for versions, **Draft version 1** for plans.
- The planner summary reads "12 (1 + 11)" and the seed band explains the split.
- Commitment copy says "short checksum", not "fingerprint".
- The fun score says it is a heuristic, not a prediction of edits.
- "A participant never gets their own track and never gets the same track
  twice."

## Wave 3 — accessibility

Reviewer: independent subagent. Must-fixes found and resolved:

- Late-seed buttons now have names that include the person.
- Extending the seed deadline focuses the date input; cancel and save restore
  focus to the trigger.
- The seed round heading is focusable and receives focus after open and close.
- Late seeds and the seeds-in count are polite live regions.
- The seed band is a named group with a heading, a real list, and visually
  hidden "owned by" text.
- Disabled actions are described by their reason text.
- The seed deadline field help is linked with `aria-describedby`.
- The planner warning link moves focus to the warning heading.
- The dialog footer wraps at narrow widths.
