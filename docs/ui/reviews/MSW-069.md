# MSW-069, MSW-070, MSW-072 — UI review record

**Scope:** flexible participation and handoff caps, host-recorded away ranges,
and open schedule disclosure.

## Captures

- `setup-review-*` — participation, schedule visibility, round deadline.
- `people-populated-*` — away range on a participant row, Away dates action.
- `people-away-dialog-*` — add and remove away ranges.
- `plan-flexible-*` — coverage gap warning and gap cells.
- `mission-join-open-*` — open schedule disclosure at join.

## Wave 1 — visual design

Must-fixes found and resolved:

- The open-schedule warning was split between a weak privacy line and a second
  box in the invite card. **Fixed:** one amber privacy warning; the invite card
  no longer repeats it.
- The away range wrapped across two lines and used low-contrast amber.
  **Fixed:** a compact one-line range at 11 px with the muted token.
- Role labels broke "Fill-in" across lines. **Fixed:** `white-space: nowrap`.
- The gap count was not actionable and the demo matrix had no empty cells.
  **Fixed:** the demo plan has two real gap cells, each marked "Gap", and the
  banner names the cause and the fill options.
- The plan's R1 band and top stack hide the matrix at 1024. **Note:** the seed
  band stays as the host contract; the matrix has a horizontal scroll region.
- "fill-ins" could break across lines. **Fixed:** non-breaking "fill-in".

## Wave 2 — UX and copy

Must-fixes found and resolved:

- "Strict full exposure" read like an anonymity setting. **Fixed:** "Full
  coverage (strict)" and "Private (own assignments)" / "Open (no anonymity)".
- The setup review could create a blocked strict event without a warning.
  **Fixed:** the participation help states the cap rule; the planner refuses
  the plan and names the person.
- The copied join instructions omitted the open-schedule warning and the cap
  requirement. **Fixed:** the copy adds the open warning, the required cap for
  strict events, or the earliest-rounds rule for flexible events.
- People, Planner, and the Away dialog claimed host-only visibility under an
  open schedule. **Fixed:** open banner on People, "Visible to everyone" in the
  seed band, and open-aware lock and away copy.
- "Active" counted paused people. **Fixed:** Ready, Seeds ready, Fill-ins,
  Paused.
- The delivery banner named no one. **Fixed:** it names the affected person.
- The away dialog failed silently on invalid dates. **Fixed:** Add is disabled
  and an inline alert explains the rule.
- Planner gap copy used "honors" and did not explain the earliest-rounds rule.
  **Fixed:** it states the cap cause, the earliest-rounds rule, and the fill
  options.
- The lock dialog claimed no owner/title/comment is revealed. **Fixed:** open
  schedule states that everyone already sees them.

## Wave 3 — accessibility

Must-fixes found and resolved:

- Away ranges were hidden at 1280 because they used the responsive-only
  `.person-meta`. **Fixed:** a permanent `.person-away` line.
- Invalid away dates were silent. **Fixed:** `aria-invalid`, linked help, an
  alert, and a disabled Add button.
- The handoff cap disappeared at 900 px. **Fixed:** the cap is repeated in the
  person cell at narrow widths and the column shows "cap / rounds".
- Setup warning tone was color-only. **Fixed:** distinct icons and a hidden
  "Danger / Warning / Note" prefix.
- The gap banner and gap cells had no accessible text. **Fixed:** "Gap" label
  and a live status banner.
- Date formats differed. **Fixed:** the away row uses a compact event-zone
  range; the dialog uses the event zone and states UTC storage.

**Remaining note:** inline refresh after pause/away replace the view with a
skeleton and move focus to the page heading. A later slice will keep the table
mounted and restore focus.
